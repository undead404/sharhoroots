# Волости и важнейшие селения Европейской России. Выпуск III

## Coverage and usefulness

Official statistical directory of volosts and significant settlements in the southern and southwestern governorates. Useful for checking the printed 1885 administrative grouping and settlement entries.

## Useful locators

- Podolia governorate, Yampol uyezd: printed p. 282, section «145. Вол. Мурафская»; entry no. 1497 identifies «Деребчинъ». The scan is available at [Internet Archive, page n147](https://archive.org/details/MostImportantVillagesEuropeanRussia/1885.Vivser_Vyp_3_Gubernii_Malorossiyskie_I_Yugo-Zapadnye_1885/page/n147/mode/2up). The matching full-text OCR is [available here](https://archive.org/stream/MostImportantVillagesEuropeanRussia/1885.Vivser_Vyp_3_Gubernii_Malorossiyskie_I_Yugo-Zapadnye_1885_djvu.txt).
- Podolia governorate, Yampol uyezd: printed pp. 284–285, «Перечень волостей по станам»; p. 285 explicitly places Murafa volost in the 2nd and 3rd police camps. [Internet Archive scan, page n148](https://archive.org/details/MostImportantVillagesEuropeanRussia/1885.Vivser_Vyp_3_Gubernii_Malorossiyskie_I_Yugo-Zapadnye_1885/page/n148/mode/2up).
- Podolia governorate, Yampol uyezd: printed pp. 282–283, entry no. 1525 lists «Дружинъ» under «147. Вол. Рожнятовская»; the county’s volost-by-camp list on p. 285 includes Rozhnyatovskaya but not a separately named Dzhuryn volost. [Internet Archive scan, pages n147–n148](https://archive.org/details/MostImportantVillagesEuropeanRussia/1885.Vivser_Vyp_3_Gubernii_Malorossiyskie_I_Yugo-Zapadnye_1885/page/n147/mode/2up).
- Podolia governorate, Mogilev uyezd: printed p. 273, section «92. Вол. Корышковская», entries nos. 976–988. The scan and OCR list the center «Корышковъ» and twelve other settlements, including «Долювцы» (Dovhivtsi) and «Перепельчинцы» (Perepilchyntsi). [Internet Archive scan, page n143](https://archive.org/details/MostImportantVillagesEuropeanRussia/1885.Vivser_Vyp_3_Gubernii_Malorossiyskie_I_Yugo-Zapadnye_1885/page/n143/mode/2up).
- Podolia governorate, Mogilev uyezd: printed p. 285 places «Корышковская» in the 3rd police camp. [Internet Archive scan, page n148](https://archive.org/details/MostImportantVillagesEuropeanRussia/1885.Vivser_Vyp_3_Gubernii_Malorossiyskie_I_Yugo-Zapadnye_1885/page/n148/mode/2up).

## Access and local copy

- The full scan and OCR are openly accessible through Internet Archive. The cited Karyshkov entries on printed p. 273 and the volost-by-camp list on printed p. 285 were checked against the scan images and OCR. No local source copy is retained.
