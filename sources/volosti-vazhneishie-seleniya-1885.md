# Волости и важнейшие селения Европейской России. Выпуск III

## Coverage and usefulness

Official statistical directory of volosts and significant settlements in the southern and southwestern governorates. Useful for checking the printed 1885 administrative grouping and settlement entries.

## Access and local copy

- The full scan and OCR are openly accessible through Internet Archive. The cited Karyshkov entries on printed p. 273, Serby volost entries on p. 274, and the volost-by-camp list on printed p. 285 were checked against the scan images. The Krasnyanskaya entries on printed pp. 282–283, its placement in the 2nd camp on p. 285, and the settlement count in the table on p. 250 were checked against the scan images. The p. 273 OCR error «Долювцы» has been distinguished from the printed reading «Долговцы». No local source copy is retained.
