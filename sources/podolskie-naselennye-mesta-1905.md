# Населенные места Подольской губернии

## Coverage and usefulness

Administrative and settlement reference for Podolia. Useful for checking which volost a named settlement was grouped under in the 1905 index.

## Useful locators

- Preface, printed pp. III–IV (PDF pp. 3–4 in the retained scan): the editor says the directory is arranged by uyezd and volost to give a «более или менее цельное понятие» of administrative divisions, and that settlements and farmsteads with at least ten households are included. This is the editor's stated scope, not a guarantee that smaller settlements or every administrative unit appear.
- Mogilev uyezd, printed p. 450: heading «3) Копайгородская волость» (PDF p. 434). This heading is absent from Familio's table of volost groups for the uyezd.
- Mogilev uyezd, printed p. 463: section «9) Сербянская волость» includes «Бѣляны Шаргородскіе» as a settlement. The retained scan's PDF p. 447 was checked against the image. This establishes the entry's placement in the directory, not the nonexistence of a separately named volost. [Familio digital index](https://familio.org/knowledge-base/catalogs/podolsk1893?level1=7e4921e0-48ff-4b36-8d09-d2d99f8ff51e&page-group=1&page-records=1&level2=98162d4a-7572-4c41-a9e0-c13d5aaa4ff3) gives the corresponding hierarchy separately.
- Yampol uyezd → Murafa volost → «Деребчин»: [Familio digital index](https://familio.org/knowledge-base/catalogs/podolsk1893?level1=c87604b0-1eeb-454b-b45b-cd2b4564c59f&page-group=1&page-records=1&level2=f67a5a9b-ea3b-411e-8491-dbb1a7058a50). The index groups Derebchyn under Murafa volost; the original printed page has not been verified.
- Yampol uyezd → Rozhnyatovka volost → «Джурин»: [Familio digital index](https://familio.org/knowledge-base/catalogs/podolsk1893?level1=c87604b0-1eeb-454b-b45b-cd2b4564c59f&level2=f90af2da-6d4d-436d-8d27-ae84a42683ae). The index places the settlement under Rozhnyatovka volost; the original printed page and displayed population/household values were not verified.

## Access and local copy

- Bibliographic record: [National Library of Ukraine catalog](https://irbis-nbuv.gov.ua/ulib/item/ukr0000027144), *Населенные места Подольской губернии*, A. P. Krylov, Kamianets-Podilskyi, 1905, VIII, 538 pages. A user-provided scan is retained at `books/2787d70693e6d2cd44bbc97134ce30c18a131e00.pdf` (542 PDF pages); its cover, preface, and printed pp. 450 and 463 have been inspected. The scan reaches printed p. 562, which differs from the catalog's extent, and does not contain a continuous copy of the Mogilev section: the page sequence jumps from printed p. 436 to 445, p. 468 to 471, and p. 474 to 477. The missing pages are needed to establish the complete section and its volost headings. The Familio index is a separate locator and does not replace those pages.
