# Research task lifecycle and completion

Read when creating, selecting, resuming, updating, or closing a research task. These conventions also define the evidence record for standalone research.

## Atomic task convention

- A task is one answerable research question with one completion outcome. It may require several sources and may target multiple articles, but it is not a list of independent questions or edits.
- Use `targets` as a YAML sequence of article paths (not a singular `target` field). List every article the findings are intended to inform and update the list as research reveals relevant existing destinations. An empty sequence records no currently identified target; assess destinations from the findings before concluding that no article is in scope.
- Keep task notes in `tasks/`, based on `tasks/TEMPLATE.md`. Task states are: `queued` before work begins; `in_progress` while research is underway and can proceed; `deferred` for an essential dependency's timed retry with a required `not_before`; `blocked` while a specific essential source/access/response is unavailable without a concrete retry time; `found` when new supported findings and their evidence are saved in the task but article-worthy findings are not yet applied; `done` after article-worthy findings are applied and the task records why other supported findings were kept out of public prose; `no_result` when bounded research found no useful supported information after alternatives were exhausted and no essential source remains pending; and `fail` when a technical failure stopped work, not when research reached a historical conclusion. `no_result` is not evidence that the searched-for fact or document does not exist. Check deferred tasks when due; resume blocked tasks as `in_progress` when the dependency is met; reopen `no_result` only for a materially different or newly available research route; retry `fail` after addressing or working around its technical cause. In the [search procedure’s access/pending-response rules](search-procedure.md), use `deferred` instead of `blocked` whenever a concrete retry time is known.
- Record web queries and the actual source documents processed in the task. For an ordinary research request without an existing task, include these in the returned evidence record; create task notes only for distinct worthwhile follow-ups.
- Reuse local source copies and recorded search coverage when available. Batch useful human search/retrieval requests under step 6; do not repeat an unanswered request without a substantive change, and do not let nonessential access failures stall the research.
- When applying findings, add Obsidian wikilinks to relevant existing notes and bold the target article's title at its natural first mention. Do not create links to nonexistent notes or add irrelevant links merely to increase link count.

## Completion check

- Target discovery has been checked against the actual findings and existing articles, even if `targets` was initially empty. Each valuable finding has a recorded destination and verified application or existing coverage, or a specific justified exclusion. A valuable finding awaiting a suitable destination keeps the task `found`; an empty target list never makes completion automatic.
- Each historical or genealogical assertion derived from research has a reference that supports that specific assertion. User-provided facts do not require independent corroboration; retain any uncertainty the user marked.
- Each research-derived article finding is locally relevant and supported. User-provided facts are treated as established under the root contract. Named people with a source-supported connection to the target territory are not excluded as low-value or insufficiently notable; external people without such a connection are not made local by their name alone.
- Negative evidence is stated only at the scope the source supports; absence from a list is not turned into proof of non-occurrence, continued status, or unchanged boundaries without an exhaustive, identity-matched comparison and consideration of alternatives.
- The locator allows a reader to find the evidence. Article prose distinguishes what a source establishes from what remains uncertain, without exposing research-process or agent-environment failures; those details are recorded in the task.
- The investigation record includes web search coverage and at least one processed source document, or explicitly records why the work remains incomplete. Resumption and application-only work reuse recorded coverage rather than repeat it. A source record alone is not a processed document.
- A task marked `found` has evidence recorded but supported local-person mentions and other article-worthy findings have not yet been applied; a task marked `done` has those mentions and findings applied and records why other supported findings failing the reader-value gate were kept out of public prose; a task marked `deferred` names the dependency, a timezone-explicit `not_before`, and the retry's timing basis; a task marked `blocked` names the essential dependency and why it matters without a known retry time; a task marked `no_result` records the bounded search, limits, and why no supported finding or useful inference emerged without implying nonexistence; a task marked `fail` records the technical error that stopped work and is not presented as a research result.
- Target article prose added or revised from findings includes appropriate Obsidian wikilinks to relevant existing notes and bolds the article title at its natural first mention.
- Before applying findings, check all new or revised reader-facing text in `content/` for Ukrainian wording and Ukrainian-script rendering of foreign personal and place names; retain another language only in exact publication titles.
- Each worthwhile unanswered follow-up discovered during research has a non-duplicative, atomic task note.
- Names, dates, places, and quoted wording in research records have not been silently normalized or inferred; reader-facing text in `content/` follows the Ukrainian-language and name-rendering rules in the root contract.
- The Ukrainian prose does not state more than the evidence supports.
