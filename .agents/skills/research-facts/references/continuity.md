# Research effort and continuity

Read on resumption, handoff, compaction, delegation, timed retry, or a change of research direction.

Optimize useful, supported findings per total agent and quota usage (across OpenCode, Codex, or Antigravity), not elapsed time. Delegation is not inherently cheaper; include briefing, inherited instructions/context, the worker's research, returned material, integration and verification. Do not claim quota savings from shorter elapsed time or fewer visible messages.

- **Retrieve selectively.** Start with filename/heading searches and relevant task checkpoints, then read matching evidence sections and source passages. Use exact locators to extract the needed pages or text windows; expand when context, contradictions or completeness require it. Avoid dumping whole queues, books, OCR runs or long logs into context. Batch independent lookups, but return only useful results. Load specialist workflows when their operation is needed, not every skill mentioned in a research log.
- **Choose the next unit of work.** Reuse an applicable established result first; do a short, tightly coupled lookup directly. Investigate an unexpected lead now when it changes the answer, resolves a consequential conflict, identifies a locally relevant person or unlocks essential evidence. Preserve worthwhile independent questions as linked atomic tasks under step 7; postpone low-value tangents without creating queue noise. Do not use a fixed search/source count as a stopping rule: continue while a concrete route could materially improve the answer, or record the specific dependency that prevents it.
- **Delegate selectively when available and permitted.** A subagent is appropriate for an independently answerable, necessary question with a clear deliverable when substantial exploratory reading would otherwise accumulate in a parent investigation that still has work to do. Keep short lookups, tightly coupled interpretation and final synthesis direct. Prefer direct work when delegation would copy most of the parent context, duplicate searches, or require similarly extensive rereading. Context isolation can help but is not evidence of lower total usage. Use a minimal brief rather than full history when supported: question and relevance, existing findings and exhausted routes, exact file/source pointers, applicable repository rules, allowed operations, evidence standard and completion condition. Do not send archival images to a model. Assign separate questions or source ranges and one owner for each edited file; avoid recursive delegation or duplicate independent passes unless their evidential value justifies the extra usage.
- **Return evidence, not a second transcript.** A delegated investigation returns its answer or specific blocker, precise supporting locators and retained-text paths, source/coverage limits, conflicts, useful searches and next unresolved step. Save durable evidence in the existing task/source mechanisms; the parent integrates the result and checks the actual supporting passages, not the worker's authority. Do not automatically repeat the worker's entire search. Delegation itself does not justify opening a new task or creating a new storage layer.
- **Checkpoint at meaningful boundaries.** Before a handoff, context compaction, pause or change of research direction, update the concise current-state block near the top of the task (see tasks/TEMPLATE.md). Point to evidence and retained readings rather than copying them; preserve conflicts, unresolved questions, coverage, exhausted routes and the next useful action. Keep historical logs and raw readings intact below or at their existing linked paths; explicitly supersede stale blockers. On resumption, read this block and relevant evidence first, expanding into the log only as needed. Operational sharing failures remain separate from historical blockers.
- **Defer a timed retry.** When an essential dependency is temporarily unavailable and there is a concrete retry time, use `status: deferred` with a quoted ISO 8601 `not_before` including the date and UTC offset; record the timing basis and any buffer. Before that instant do not probe the resource. Once due, `start-task` treats the task alongside `in_progress`, after `found` and before `queued` and checks access; a due time is not proof of restored access. On success use `in_progress` and remove the time gate; on failure either set a justified later retry or use `blocked` if no retry time is known. This is queue eligibility, not an automatic launch. Follow `.opencode/commands/start-task.md` for selection and retry details.

For example, comparing the Murafa commission's two allowance amounts calls first for checking the recorded currency evidence and the exact nominal relationship. Purchasing power in a particular place/month is a different question; pursue it only if it materially informs interpretation. A substantial necessary currency investigation can be isolated; a single applicable source check normally stays direct. Neither route may turn nominal equivalence into market equivalence or reconcile conflicting event dates by assumption.
