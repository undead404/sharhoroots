# Place Jewish community material

Use this skill when routing material involving Jewish people, communities, records, institutions, or Judaic practice, including when editing or extracting material from a `*-judaic.md` article. Apply [content-placement](../content-placement/SKILL.md) for the general rules on settlement and institutional scope, record coverage, extraction, and record discovery.

## Choose the primary article

Articles titled «Єврейське товариство …» cover Jewish communities as historical entities across religious and secular life. Apply this scope across periods and governing authorities, including communities without formal legal recognition. Preserve the existing titles, filenames, and public URLs. Use the community article as the primary home for the following subjects.

- Confession-specific vital registration before universal secular civil registration, including Jewish metrical books and their archival holdings. Include population lists and other genealogical sources specific to Jews.
- Religious life, including synagogues, prayer houses, rabbis, religious education, cemeteries, and burial societies.
- Communal organization, self-government, charities and associations, secular education, cultural activities, and political organizations. Include Jewish national administrative entities, agricultural communes, and other specifically Jewish collective institutions.
- The treatment of Jews as a distinct legal, administrative, ethnic, religious, or cultural group, including privileges, restrictions, obligations, taxation, and discrimination.
- Antisemitic persecution, pogroms, ghettos, deportations, and the Holocaust.
- The community's formation, development, transformation, decline, and disappearance.

Keep settlement-wide history in the settlement article, including demographic breakdowns and activities of Jewish residents when their identity is incidental to the documented subject. Place universal civil registration and general population records under the general rules.

When an institution already has a dedicated page, use it for the institution's detailed history and records. Give the Jewish community article the relevant context and a link. Apply the general extraction criteria to schools, synagogues, Jewish national village councils, communes, ghettos, and other distinct entities. Keep community-specific institutional detail in the community article while a dedicated institutional page is unwarranted.

## Keep both contexts useful

When an event matters to both the settlement and the Jewish community, keep a concise, sourced summary in both contexts. Link any summary outside the primary article to the detailed account. Name Jewish metrical books and other relevant Jewish-specific record categories on the settlement page and link to the corresponding inventory or section where practical.

## Placement examples

Paths are relative to `content/`.

- In `settlement/Sharhorod.md`, keep city population figures and their demographic breakdowns. Put kahal counts covering attached settlements in `registrar/Sharhorod-judaic.md` and label their coverage. A resident's property dispute with the monastery can inform both city life and the monastery's property history. Consider the community article if the dispute also concerns the Jewish community as such.
- For `settlement/Murafa.md` and `settlement/Nova-Murafa.md`, retain sourced summaries of locally significant pogroms. Put detailed accounts and victim lists in the relevant community article when its coverage is established. Check which place and community the source documents before assigning earlier events to later communities or using proprietary divisions to infer registrar jurisdiction.
